How to Play

Objective: Move all discs from the first rod to the last rod.

Rules:

  • Only one disc can be moved at a time
  • A larger disc cannot be placed on a smaller disc
  • You must move all discs to the rightmost rod

Player 1 Controls:

  • Q - Select Left Rod
  • W - Select Middle Rod
  • E - Select Right Rod
  • Space - Pick/Place Disc

Player 2 Controls:

  • U - Select Left Rod
  • I - Select Middle Rod
  • O - Select Right Rod
  • Enter - Pick/Place Disc

Scoring: Finish in fewer moves and less time to score more points!
